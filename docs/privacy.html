Hearth

Privacy

Last updated 25 November 2026

Short version: Hearth runs on your PC. There is no Hearth account, no Hearth server, no analytics and no tracking. Hearth only talks to the services listed below, and only to do what you asked.

What Hearth connects to

ServiceWhyWhat is sent
Nexus ModsBrowsing mods, downloads, collections, update checksYour Nexus Mods API key or sign-in, and the ids of the mods and files you look at or download. The built-in browser is the Nexus Mods website itself, and its cookies stay on your PC.
GitHubChecking for Hearth updates; downloading the LOOT masterlist for load-order sortingA normal web request for the update file or masterlist. No personal details.
Steam's image serverGame artworkA request for a game's public header image.

Mods themselves come from the download servers Nexus Mods hands Hearth for each file.

What Hearth stores on your PC

  • Your Nexus Mods credentials are kept in Windows Credential Manager, never in Hearth's own files or logs.
  • Settings, your mod list, profiles and downloads are kept in Hearth's folder under %APPDATA%\Hearth.
  • Log files help with troubleshooting. They stay on your PC and are never uploaded.
  • The built-in browser keeps its cookies and cache in Hearth's local app-data folder, like any browser.

Uninstalling Hearth removes the program. To remove your data too, delete %APPDATA%\Hearth, and remove the Hearth entries from Windows Credential Manager.

Earlier versions offered Ask Hearth, an optional AI assistant. It has been removed. If you saved a key for it, you can delete the 'Hearth Mod Manager' entries ending in '-api-key' in Windows Credential Manager. Keep the nexus-api-key entry for Nexus Mods. To remove old chats, delete the assistant folder inside %APPDATA%\Hearth.

Problem reports

"Report a problem" creates a zip file on your PC. Before saving it, Hearth removes your Windows user name, folder paths, computer name, email addresses and keys, and shows you exactly what is included. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to share the file yourself.

What Hearth never does

  • It never sells, shares or collects your data, and there is no telemetry.
  • It never changes your game folder until you turn a mod on, and it can put the folder back the way it was.
  • It never clicks downloads on Nexus Mods for you.
